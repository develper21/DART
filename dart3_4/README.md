# DART-3.4

Multi-regime task-law discovery.

DART-3.3 showed strong invariance on discovery regimes A/B but failure on a third unseen regime. DART-3.4 discovers the task program across four independent regimes A/B/C/D and reserves a fifth regime E as an untouched final test.

A candidate must remain good and causally consistent across the discovery regimes instead of winning from a single validation distribution.
