# DART-1.8 Theory

DART-1.7 showed that a typed interface can exhibit source-task invariance, but similarity of representations does not establish computational necessity.

DART-1.8 separates three questions:

1. **Sufficiency:** does a reduced representation preserve the teacher computation?
2. **Necessity:** does removing a selected feature cause a measurable behavioral loss?
3. **Transfer:** does the same causally necessary bottleneck remain useful on an unseen related task?

The target is a minimum intermediate computational subgraph, not merely a low-dimensional embedding.
